Executive Summary / Incamake

Rwandans arriving in Germany (students, vocational training Azubi, and professionals) face high administrative friction with Anmeldung, health insurance, and visa processing. Experienced community leaders possess the knowledge but face overload answering repetitive queries. Ejo Labs proposes deploying an Agentic AI Assistant that captures the knowledge of community advisors, organizes it into a RAG knowledge base, and resolves onboarding inquiries 24/7 in natural Kinyarwanda, English, and German via web or WhatsApp.

Scaling Community Intelligence

Bureaucratic friction shouldn't slow down the onboarding of new arrivals. Ejo Labs builds custom vector-grounded AI middleware that connects RCAG's collective expertise directly to WhatsApp.

The Bureaucratic Friction

Every semester, hundreds of new students and Azubi arrive in Germany. Administrative knowledge (e.g., registration, Ausbildung paperwork, local city laws) is locked in the heads of senior members, creating a communication bottleneck and causing leadership burnout.

The Agentic RAG Solution

Ejo Labs compiles advisor-approved documentation into a vector database (Qdrant). A fine-tuned open-source model references this local knowledge base to deliver instant, legally compliant, and context-aware checklists.

Core System Capabilities

  • Audio Voice Input: Allows new arrivals to ask questions via voice notes in Kinyarwanda, transcribing and processing the local dialect with high accuracy.
  • Mentor-Led Knowledge Feeding: Administrators can update the database simply by sending a text or voice message to a designated admin bot, ensuring the knowledge grows dynamically.
  • Secure & Grounded Answers: Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) tied to official German guidelines, preventing any false administrative advice.
