The Challenge
One person cannot help a hundred.

Rwandans arriving in Germany — students, Azubi, young professionals — face a wall of unfamiliar processes. City registration (Anmeldung), health insurance, residence permits, and bank accounts. The information exists, but it lives in the heads of a few experienced community members who are already stretched thin.

When one person helps another navigate the Anmeldung, that knowledge disappears after the call. The next person starts from zero. And the person helping cannot scale — one call at a time, one person at a time.

Knowledge stuck in calls
Valuable guidance stays between two people and is never captured or reused.
Same questions, every time
Community leaders answer the same questions repeatedly with no way to scale.
Help isn't always available
Newcomers need answers at odd hours, on weekends, in moments of stress.

Our Proposal
A knowledge base that grows with your community.

Ejo Labs proposes to build an AI assistant for RCA — embedded in your website and WhatsApp — that captures the lived knowledge of your community members and makes it available to every new arrival, instantly, in the language they're most comfortable in.

Every time a community member helps someone navigate a process, we document it clearly and add it to the assistant's knowledge base. The assistant gets smarter with every interaction. The community leader helps one person — and that help reaches hundreds more.

Concrete Example
How Anmeldung goes from one call to a community resource
1
Leader Guides Arrival

They walk a newcomer through booking a registration appointment and the needed documents.

2
Ejo Structures Info

We document the advice into a step-by-step vector-grounded Q&A file.

3
Arrivals Ask Assistant

New arrivals ask questions via Web/WhatsApp in Kinyarwanda or English 24/7.

4
Base Grows Constantly

Insurance, bank setup, and contracts are added, preventing repeated questions.

The Result: Mentors' time is saved for complex, high-priority issues that need genuine human support.

What We Need From RCA
A small commitment, to start.

Access to knowledge: Short recorded/written onboarding advice sessions with community mentors.

Common questions: List of top topics newcomers ask about to prioritize database ingestion.

A digital home: Integration with website/WhatsApp (Ejo Labs handles the technical setup completely).

Ongoing feedback: Community review as we test and improve the assistant during initial phases.
