The Challenge
One person cannot help a hundred.

Rwandans arriving in Germany — students, Azubi, young professionals — face a wall of unfamiliar processes. City registration, health insurance, residence permits, bank accounts. The information exists, but it lives in the heads of a few experienced community members who are already stretched thin.

When one person helps another navigate the Anmeldung, that knowledge disappears after the call. The next person starts from zero. And the person helping cannot scale — one call at a time, one person at a time.

📞
Knowledge stuck in calls
Valuable guidance stays between two people and is never captured or reused.
🔁
Same questions, every time
Community leaders answer the same questions repeatedly with no way to scale their reach.
🕐
Help isn't always available
Newcomers need answers at odd hours, on weekends, in moments of stress.
Our Proposal
A knowledge base that grows with your community.

Ejo Labs proposes to build an AI assistant for RCA — embedded in your website and WhatsApp — that captures the lived knowledge of your community members and makes it available to every new arrival, instantly, in the language they're most comfortable in.

Every time a community member helps someone navigate a process, we document it clearly and add it to the assistant's knowledge base. The assistant gets smarter with every interaction. The community leader helps one person — and that help reaches hundreds more.

Concrete Example
How city registration (Anmeldung) goes from one call to a community resource
1
A community leader helps someone register

They walk a new arrival through booking an Anmeldung appointment online, what documents to bring, what to expect at the Bürgeramt, and common mistakes to avoid.

2
Ejo Labs documents and structures that knowledge

We turn the guidance into a clear, step-by-step document — including links, tips, and city-specific differences — and add it to the assistant's knowledge base.

3
The next person asks the assistant

A student arriving in Berlin asks: "How do I register my address?" The assistant answers — in Kinyarwanda, English, or French — with the exact guidance, step by step, 24/7.

4
The knowledge base keeps growing

Health insurance, Ausbildung enrollment, opening a bank account, understanding a German contract — each topic added means one fewer call, one less person left struggling alone.

The result: The community leader's expertise reaches not just one person — but every Rwandan who arrives after them. Their time is freed for the cases that genuinely need human support.
