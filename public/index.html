Skilling & Awareness: Empowering Communities

AI adoption is an educational challenge, not just a technical one.

Surveys in Rwanda show that while many citizens have heard about Artificial Intelligence, very few know how to use it practically to solve daily challenges. We are bridging this gap through hands-on education and community outreach.

Our youth-to-community flywheel builds a generation of young AI advocates first. Through the free EjoChat API, developer workshops, and hackathons, we empower local youth who in turn introduce these practical tools to their families and elders.
